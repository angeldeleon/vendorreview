BRIGHTWAVE INC.

SOC 2 Type II Report
Report on Controls Relevant to Security and Confidentiality

Independent Service Auditor's Report

Scope

We have examined Brightwave Inc.'s description of its Webinar Platform system
for the period from 2024-01-01 to 2024-12-31, and the suitability of the design
and operating effectiveness of controls stated in the description throughout
the period from 2024-01-01 to 2024-12-31 to meet the applicable trust services
criteria for Security and Confidentiality.

The trust services criteria in scope of this examination are Security and
Confidentiality. Availability, Processing Integrity and Privacy were not
included in the scope of this report.

Brightwave Inc. uses a subservice organisation for cloud infrastructure
hosting. The description includes only the controls of Brightwave Inc. and
excludes the controls of the subservice organisation. Our examination did not
extend to controls of the subservice organisation. This is the carve-out
method.

Opinion

In our opinion, in all material respects, the controls stated in the
description were suitably designed and operated effectively throughout the
period. This is an unqualified opinion.

Results of Tests of Controls

CC6.1 Logical access — no exceptions noted.
CC6.2 User registration — no exceptions noted.
CC6.3 Role-based access — exceptions were noted. For 2 of 25 terminations
      sampled, access removal exceeded the documented five business day target.
      Management response: the terminations were contractors offboarded through
      a manual process which has since been automated.
CC7.2 System monitoring — no exceptions noted.
CC8.1 Change management — no exceptions noted.
